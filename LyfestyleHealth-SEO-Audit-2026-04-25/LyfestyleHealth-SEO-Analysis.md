## Dimension 1: Crawlability & Indexation — 2/10

**Score Justification:** Google's site: query returns only 2 indexed pages (homepage and /collections). The primary page provided for audit — /apps/our-menu — is generated by a Shopify third-party app and may carry a noindex directive or be excluded from crawling. A 5-year-old local business should have 20+ indexed pages minimum.

**Findings:**

| Issue | Impact | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Only 2 pages indexed by Google | High | site:lyfestylehealthbar.com returns homepage + /collections only | Create indexable Shopify pages for menu, about, location, FAQ |
| Menu at /apps/our-menu not indexed | High | /apps/ path not in Google index; Shopify app pages frequently carry noindex or JS-render issues | Migrate menu to native Shopify page at /pages/menu |
| robots.txt not verified | High | 403 on direct access — unclear what is blocked | Confirm robots.txt is accessible and not blocking key paths |
| sitemap.xml not verified | Medium | 403 on direct access — Google may not have a valid sitemap to crawl | Submit a valid sitemap via Google Search Console |
| No blog, no location pages, no FAQ in index | High | site: query shows only 2 pages | Create content pages; each creates an indexable URL |

## Dimension 4: Content Quality & E-E-A-T — 4/10

The website has no visible blog, no story page in the index, no sourced claims, and no educational content. The brand story is compelling but invisible to search engines.

**E-E-A-T Assessment:**

Experience: Partial. Hands-on expertise in juice bar/wellness is evident from product range and social content, but the website does not demonstrate first-hand experience through written content.

Expertise: Partial. All-natural, organic, no artificial syrups messaging suggests expertise, but no sourced nutritional information, no blog posts, and no author credentials are present on the site.

Authoritativeness: Weak. 26 Yelp reviews for a 5-year-old business is low. The brand is listed in Yelp's top 10 acai bowls in Anaheim but has minimal organic web presence to build authority signals.

Trustworthiness: Partial. HTTPS is present. Business address and phone are findable via Yelp. No privacy policy, terms, or About page is confirmed in the Google index.
