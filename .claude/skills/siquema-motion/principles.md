# Principles

The whole job is saying no. When a choice is unclear, these decide it.

1. **Evidence, not effects.** Every motion has to show something true: the real
   tub, the real scoop, the real workout, the real label. If a move only makes the
   screen busier, cut it.

2. **Real over rendered.** Filmed beats generated. A shaky phone clip of the real
   scoop beats a perfect 3D tub. Generated footage is allowed only for
   backgrounds and atmosphere, never for the product, never for people presented
   as customers.

3. **In the scene, not on top.** Type and graphics live in the space: on the
   wall, behind the athlete, on the label, tracking the camera. Stickers floating
   on top of the video come last, and get used sparingly.

4. **Receipts on every claim.** Every number and every claim on screen has a
   source in `sources.json`, and the source line can be shown on screen. No
   source, no number.

5. **Every piece moves the language forward.** Each reel should try one move
   that's `new` or an evolution of a `current` move. Don't just repeat what
   worked last week. And don't chase a trend that's already everywhere (`watch`).

6. **Loud, not cheap.** SÍ QUEMA is loud gym energy. Loud means hard cuts on the
   beat, big type, real sweat, bold color fields. Cheap means glow, sparkles,
   shaking, flashing red, numbers spinning up. The energy comes from the edit and
   the footage, not from effects.
