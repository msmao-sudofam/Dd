## 2026-11-05 · Animation Studio, promo reference
my note:        (sent the promo recording) "come up with ur own ideas" · "Allow cartoon characters" · "All 10"
what it learned: The promo sells STORY, not information: one hero doing one spectacular action (a car that lifts off), in a full world (skyline, sky, ground), with characters reacting (a crowd, a sign), told identically in every style, with almost no text. Build ads that way: one story, a hero action, environment, a reacting crowd, one label line at the end. Cartoon characters are allowed (compliance.md).
