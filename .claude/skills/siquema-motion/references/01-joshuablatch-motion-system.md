# 01 · @joshuablatch: "my Claude motion system" reel
source:   TikTok @joshuablatch, 77s vertical screen recording (uploaded 2026-10-19)
added:    2026-10-19
why M Mo likes it: it's the system this skill is built on. Taste written down, a gate that says no, moves with a shelf life.

## breakdown
**Density over cuts**: something new every ~2 seconds in one color field.
The background stays put; the content changes on it.

**Print-on-page flicker**: stills flash inside a still paper page, with a tiny
brand line. The frame is static; the images inside it swap fast.

**Breathe then burst**: 4–6 s of talking head, then quick inserts on every word.
Slow, then fast, then slow again.

**Strike-through list**: banned items appear as a serif list and get struck
through with a yellow line, one by one.

**Highlighter reveal**: one phrase gets a flat yellow highlight block
("Evidence, *not effects.*"). No glow, no animation on the block other than it
appearing.

**Mono notes**: small monospace "my note / what it learned" cards. They read as
real documentation, not as motion graphics.

**Big number with its source**: an 88% sits still on a yellow card, with the
source line in small type under it. The number does not count up.

**Camera on a real screen**: the whole reel is a phone filming a monitor. Real
over rendered. The moiré and the desk show it's real.

## what he says (transcribed 2026-10-20)
- "Most AI graphics look the same right now: glowing bodies, orbit rings, or random
  floating numbers. In health, that's exactly what a scam ad looks like."
- Claude gets the brand folders, fonts, and photography first. Every animation he likes
  goes into an inspo folder, and Claude breaks it down before building anything.
- Every note on a draft is written down with the reason, "so I never have to say it twice."
- **A separate "motion director" mod whose whole job is to say no. He calls it the godfather
  of graphics.** → in this skill, the godfather is a separate reviewer agent (SKILL.md step 5).
- "Every graphic has to point to something real within the brand: a report, the product,
  the person, or even the room it's being filmed in."
- Scam tells list (glow, bounces, glitches, red hotspots): use one and it fails automatically.
  The list evolves as design evolves.
- The gate reads the code, then checks every frame **for stutters** → gate.py stutter check.
- Every approved graphic has a shelf life. Text-behind-head is in every app, so it's on watch.
  About 70 graphics tracked; when he's over one, it's retired. His call.

## rules taken
- Something new every ~2 s, but the frame/background stays calm.
- One accent color used like a highlighter, nowhere else.
- Numbers appear finished, with their source.
- Real documentation shown on screen is more convincing than motion graphics.
