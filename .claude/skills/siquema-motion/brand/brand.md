# SÍ QUEMA — brand

**What it is:** DTC supplement brand. Thermogenic powders. Shopify store at
siquemasupplements.com (storefront being rebuilt; moving to a new Shopify store).
Separate from AGENCÉ. Never mix the two stores or their assets.

**Products** (prices go through `sources.json` like any other number):
- Mango, $29.99
- Kiwi Strawberry, $29.99

**Positioning:** bilingual and Latino-led, but built for everyone. Latino identity
is the brand's soul. Loud, gym-brand energy. A real gym, not a lab or a spa.

**Voice:**
- Loud, confident, short. Sounds like someone from the gym talking, not a
  supplement company.
- Spanish and English sit side by side. Spanglish is fine when it sounds natural.
  Never machine-translate.
- No corporate-speak, no AI tone. Read every line out loud before it ships.
- The name is a promise about **heat and intensity** ("Sí, quema." Yes, it burns).
  Use the burn of the workout and the energy of the drink. Never "it burns fat".
