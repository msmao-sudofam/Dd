03 — Questions

Ask us
anything.

Straight answers, including the ones that don’t sell tubs.

How much caffeine is in it?

200 mg per serving — roughly two cups of coffee. Do not combine it with other sources of caffeine: no coffee, energy drinks or pre-workout on the days you take it. If caffeine and you don’t get along, skip this one.

When should I take it?

One scoop a day, never more. Take it in the morning, or about 30 minutes before you train, and not within six hours of sleep — the caffeine is real and it will keep you up.

Am I going to sweat?

Yes, noticeably, and you should know that going in. That’s the gamma-butyrobetaine. It’s a side effect, not a measurement — sweating is not the same thing as burning fat, and any brand telling you otherwise is selling you a feeling.

Can I take it with my pre-workout?

No. Most pre-workouts carry 200 to 350 mg of caffeine on their own, and the label says not to combine Sí Quema with other sources of caffeine. Take one or the other, not both.

Do I still have to diet?

Yes. This supports a reduced-calorie diet and regular training — it does not replace either one, and nothing sold legally does. Individual results vary and depend on what you eat and how you train.

Who shouldn’t take this?

Anyone under 18. Do not use it if you are pregnant or nursing, or sensitive to caffeine. If you have high blood pressure, a heart, thyroid, liver or kidney condition, diabetes, anxiety or a seizure disorder, or you take any medication, talk to your doctor first and take the label with you. Stop using it if you have any adverse reaction.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

Thirty days, full refund, and you keep the tub. Send one message — no form, no return shipping, no questions about why.

Which flavor should I get?

Identical formula, identical doses in all four. Mango is rounder and a little sweet; Watermelon is light and cold; Kiwi Strawberry is the tart, loud one; Cucumber Lime is savory and sharp. Can’t decide? The variety pack is one of each.
