# Costco Price-Tag Cheat Sheet

The codes Warehouse Runner reads for you. These are **decoded conventions** — Costco does not
print their meanings anywhere; shoppers and employees worked them out over decades.

## Price endings

| Ending | Meaning | Deal level |
|--------|---------|:----------:|
| **`.99`** | Standard everyday price — **not** a markdown | ⚪ none |
| **`.97`** | Clearance / manager markdown from regular price | 🟢 good |
| **`.49` / `.79`** | Temporary manufacturer deal (limited-time) | 🟡 decent |
| **`.88`** | Manager's closeout — deep markdown (also used for some Asian goods near Lunar New Year, 8 = lucky) | 🔴 deep |
| **`.00`** | Manager's final closeout — often the deepest markdown in the store | 🔴 deepest |

## Symbols

| Symbol | Nickname | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **`*` (asterisk, top-right of tag)** | "Death star" | Item will **not be restocked** — once it's gone, it's gone. Buy now if you want it. |

## Quick rule of thumb
- See **`.97`, `.88`, `.00`** → it's marked down. The lower/rounder the ending, the deeper the cut.
- See an **asterisk** → discontinued locally; last-chance combined with a markdown ending = best time to buy.
- **`.99`** → regular price; use Warehouse Runner's price history to decide buy-now vs. wait.
