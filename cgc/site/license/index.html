Certificates of insurance

General contractors and owners can request certificates of insurance for a specific project. Email nathan@cgcontractorsinc.com with the project name and the certificate holder’s details.

§ 05

Check any contractor’s license

California law requires anyone who performs construction work of $1,000 or more (labor and materials combined) to hold a license from the Contractors State License Board. You can check any contractor’s license, bond and workers’ compensation status, and any complaints or disciplinary history, at www.cslb.ca.gov or by calling 800-321-CSLB (2752).
